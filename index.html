A NOTE ON THIS EXPLORATION

Stone, not certainty.

This is an artistic, spatial interpretation of the Kailasa Temple precinct at Ellora, Cave 16 — not a survey-grade reconstruction.

The courtyard uses a commonly reported approximate 82 × 46 metre footprint. Other proportions, rock surfaces, small shrines and column profiles are inferred. Animal friezes are stylized; relief registers are abstract, not reproductions of the Ramayana, Mahabharata or Ravana shaking Kailasa panels.

No intact lateral gallery bridges, original paint scheme, other Ellora caves or invented inscriptions have been added. Free-flight navigation intentionally permits passage through stone so that you cannot become trapped.
